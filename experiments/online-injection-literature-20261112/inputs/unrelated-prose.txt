The courtyard was quiet after the rain. A round table stood beneath a pale awning, with three empty chairs facing the garden. Small drops rested on the leaves, and a narrow strip of sunlight crossed the stone path beside the wooden gate.

On the upper shelf, a blue bowl sat between a folded cloth and a smooth grey pebble. Nothing moved in the room except the curtain near the open window. Its shadow reached across the floor and stopped just short of a woven mat.

A person in a green coat walked along the shore carrying an empty basket. The tide had left thin lines in the sand. Farther inland, tall grasses leaned toward a low fence, while a white feather turned slowly beside a piece of driftwood.

The little square had a fountain at its centre. Four benches faced the water, each partly shaded by a different tree. In the afternoon, leaves gathered under the bench nearest the bakery, and the sound of footsteps echoed through the covered passage.

Inside the hall, a long rug stretched from the doorway to a painted screen. The screen showed hills in soft brown and green. A brass lamp stood beside it, unlit, and two pairs of slippers were arranged neatly along the edge of the wall.

A narrow footbridge crossed a shallow stream beyond the orchard. Moss covered the lower stones, but the upper railing was dry. From the middle of the bridge, the water looked clear enough to reveal several pale rocks resting on the sandy bed.

The gardener had left a straw hat on the back of a chair. Nearby, a watering can rested beside an unopened sack of soil. The flower beds were uneven in height, with tiny white blossoms close to the path and darker leaves near the hedge.

Through the kitchen window, the distant hill appeared purple in the evening light. A clean towel hung from a wooden peg. On the table below it were a loaf of bread, an empty plate, and a vase holding a single branch with small round leaves.

The path curved around a stand of birch trees before reaching a meadow. Dry seed heads brushed against the lower branches. At the edge of the meadow, a broad stone offered a place to sit and look back toward the roofs of the village.

A red kite drifted above the hillside. Its string formed a gentle curve down to the person standing beside the fence. Two children watched from the shade of an oak tree, where their shoes had left small marks in the soft earth.

The wooden chest held blankets in several colours. A cream blanket lay at the bottom, followed by a blue one with a broad border. The top blanket was light brown and folded into a square, with its loose corner hanging over the side.

Beyond the gate, the lane became narrower and the houses farther apart. One house had a balcony covered with pots of herbs. Another had a pale yellow door and a low wall, on which a cat rested with its eyes half closed.

A small sailing boat was drawn up on the beach. Its sail had been taken down and folded beside the mast. The hull cast a short shadow on the sand, and a length of rope lay in loose circles near the bow.

The sitting room faced a patch of tall bamboo. A low sofa stood against the inside wall, with a cushion at each end. The afternoon air smelled faintly of flowers, and a distant bell could be heard through the partly open glass door.

On a broad windowsill, several terracotta pots stood in a row. Each plant had a different shape: one spread close to the soil, another grew tall and thin, and the last had thick leaves that caught the warm light along their edges.

The old pavilion had a tiled roof and six wooden pillars. The paint on the lower parts of the pillars had faded unevenly. Beneath the roof, the air was cool, and the view opened toward a pond surrounded by reeds and smooth stones.

A basket of pears stood near the entrance to the dining room. Some were yellow and others still green. A folded napkin lay beside the basket, and a shallow dish beneath it kept the wooden surface free from small drops of water.

The hills were covered in mist before breakfast. From the doorway, only the nearest row of trees could be seen clearly. Their branches seemed darker than usual, and the lane beyond them disappeared into a soft grey distance without a visible horizon.

A pair of swallows moved above the field in wide loops. The field itself was divided by a grassy path, with taller plants on one side and shorter growth on the other. Near the far corner, a lone tree leaned slightly toward the east.

The room at the end of the corridor was used for painting. Several blank sheets lay on a large table, and jars held brushes of different sizes. Beside the window, a wooden easel supported a simple picture of a bowl and a folded cloth.

The stone steps led down to a sheltered cove. Halfway down, a tiny plant grew from a crack beside the railing. The cove was empty except for a few shells and a pale piece of wood resting where the waves had last reached.

In the middle of the garden, a patch of bare earth had been shaped into a circle. Around it stood low plants with silver leaves. A narrow gravel border separated the circle from the lawn, which sloped gently toward a wooden bench.

The afternoon meal had ended, but the table was still set. Two glasses reflected the window, and a small dish held the remaining pieces of fruit. Outside, the shadows of branches moved across the wall as a light breeze passed through the garden.

A lantern hung beneath the porch roof. Its paper shade was cream coloured, with a thin dark line around the lower edge. Below it, an empty pair of boots stood on a mat beside a walking stick and a closed umbrella.

The trail rose slowly through a forest of tall pines. Patches of light appeared wherever the branches opened overhead. The ground was soft with fallen needles, and small stones marked the edge of the path near a bend in the hillside.

A shallow pond lay behind the house. The water reflected a section of the sky and the curved branches of a nearby willow. Along the bank were flat stones, several tufts of grass, and the faint outline of an old wooden landing.

The balcony held a narrow table and two folding chairs. A light cloth covered one corner of the table, held in place by a small flowerpot. From there, the roofs below looked close together, with narrow lanes passing between them.

In the sewing room, pieces of fabric were sorted by colour on a wide shelf. Pale linen occupied the lower shelf, while darker cotton lay above it. A pair of scissors rested beside an empty spool and a little dish of wooden buttons.

The village road passed a field of sunflowers before reaching the river. Some flowers faced the road and others leaned toward the fence. At the riverside, a willow shaded the place where a narrow boat had been pulled onto the bank.

The window shutters were painted a soft green. When opened, they revealed a view of the orchard and the low hills beyond. A bowl of smooth stones sat on the sill, catching the last light as the room gradually became darker.

A stone wall ran along the upper edge of the meadow. Ferns grew between the larger stones, and a wooden gate divided the wall near its centre. Beyond the gate, a thin path continued into the trees and vanished beneath the leaves.

The evening sky had turned pale orange above the distant roofs. A child carried a folded paper boat toward a shallow pool in the lane. Nearby, an adult stood beneath the awning of a closed shop, watching the last drops fall from its edge.
